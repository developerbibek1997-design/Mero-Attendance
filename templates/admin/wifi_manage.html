How to find your BSSID

Connect to the office WiFi on your computer, then:

  • Windows: Open Command Prompt, type netsh wlan show interfaces and press Enter. Look for "BSSID".
  • Mac: Hold the Option key on your keyboard and click the WiFi icon at the top of your screen. Look for "BSSID".
