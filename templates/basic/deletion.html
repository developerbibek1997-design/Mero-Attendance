Account Deletion Request

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your Mero Attendance account and all associated personal data, please follow the steps below. Because Mero Attendance is an enterprise application, your account deletion must be verified to ensure no active employment records or payroll data are prematurely destroyed.

Step-by-Step Deletion Process:

  1. Send an email from your registered account email address to support@meroattendance.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - [Your Full Name]".
  3. In the body of the email, include your full name, the name of your organization, and your employee ID (if applicable).

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon verification of your request, we will permanently delete the following information from our active databases within 30 days:

  • Your personal profile information (Name, Email, Phone Number, Profile Picture).
  • Your login credentials and authentication tokens.
  • Your active device ID pairings.

What Data Will Be Retained?

Because Mero Attendance is a B2B Human Resources platform, certain data must be retained to comply with local labor laws and your employer's record-keeping requirements. We will securely retain the following data on behalf of your organization:

  • Historical attendance logs (Check-in/Check-out times and GPS locations).
  • Approved and rejected leave applications.
  • Generated payroll and payslip records.

If you wish to have this historical employment data completely purged, you must contact your organization's HR administrator directly, as they are the legal data controllers of your employment records.
