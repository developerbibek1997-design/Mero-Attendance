Our Mission & Vision

We started Mero Attendance to fix a simple frustration: attendance data never flows anywhere useful. We built the system we wished existed.

Our Mission

To make attendance, HRMS, payroll, billing, reporting and organization management simple, digital, transparent and affordable for schools, offices, institutes, colleges and businesses of all sizes — eliminating manual processes and spreadsheet dependency.

Our Vision

To become Nepal's most trusted cloud attendance and HRMS platform and expand as a complete organization management system that serves schools, corporates, NGOs and government institutions — both nationally and internationally.

Why Choose Us

Built differently, for organizations like yours

We didn't build a generic HR tool. We built a platform specifically shaped around how Nepali schools, offices and institutions actually operate.

Nepali Calendar Support

Full Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar integration alongside English (AD) date — switchable per organization preference. Built for Nepal first.

Biometric Device Support

Sync ZKTeco and Hikvision devices using the Puller app. Attendance flows from the machine directly into the cloud dashboard without manual import.

Staff & Student in One

Manage both employees and students/members in the same system — with separate workflows, dashboards and reports for each type.

Payroll Automation

Generate salary sheets from attendance data, calculate PF/SSF/TDS automatically and produce printable payslips without manual calculations.

Multi-Branch Ready

Manage 1 or 50 branches from one admin panel. Staff, attendance, finance and reports stay organized per branch with central oversight.

Cloud Access, No Setup

Log in from any device — desktop, tablet or mobile. No software to install, no server to maintain. Your data is always available and secure.

Mobile-Friendly Dashboard

The dashboard is fully responsive — staff can check attendance, apply for leave and view payslips from their phone without any app download.

Complete School ERP

Student admission, classification, billing, results and guardian communication — all built into the same platform as your HR and payroll workflows.

Customizable Per Org Type

Enable or disable modules per organization. A school uses billing and results. An office uses payroll. A factory uses shifts. One system, many setups.
