The Product

About Mero Attendance

One cloud platform for attendance, HR, payroll and institution management.

Mero Attendance is an all-in-one, cloud-based attendance and human resource management platform. It records attendance through whichever method suits your organization — biometric device, QR code, GPS, WiFi, facial recognition or manual entry — and carries that data straight through to leave balances, payroll, reports and management dashboards without any re-keying.

Because it is cloud-based, there is no server to buy and no software to install on each machine. Administrators work from any browser; staff and students use the mobile app or any phone browser. Every organization's data is fully isolated, and access is controlled by granular role-based permissions.
